# Duck Tracks

Footprints stay where you walked.

## Stamped on the actual step

The mod watches the height of the foot bones every frame and stamps only at the moment a foot **lands**. It is not spaced by distance, so it never drifts out of step with the walk — stand still and nothing appears, run and the prints spread out, and left and right alternate properly.

The shape defaults to **the foot you are actually wearing**. Change footwear and the print changes with it.

## Make it yours

Change the shape of the print. Pick from 21 built-in ones, drop in a PNG, draw your own, or roll a random one.

Pick any colour you like. Make prints blink, cycle through colours, or glow in the dark.

Prints can kick up particles as you step, and keep giving them off while they linger.

Size and lifetime are yours too — including keeping prints forever.

## Also

- Everything is under `Footprint Settings` in the pause menu.
